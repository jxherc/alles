# Stage 2 — Flesh and Blood

- **Status:** historical
- **Period:** June 19–21, 2026
- **Meaning:** the skeleton filled out with real feature depth

This stage tried to replace more standalone services with deeper Alles apps. It added shared primitives,
expanded Docs, Finance, Mail, Files, Photos, Calendar, Passwords, Aide, mobile support, and several new
personal apps.

The historical task ledger marks the individual tasks complete. Later stages still replaced or removed
some of the resulting product choices.

Afterlife keeps useful data and proven capabilities, but it does not keep the old one-app-per-feature
navigation or assume every completed microtask still describes the best product design.
