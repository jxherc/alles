# Stage 1 — The Skeleton

- **Status:** historical
- **Period:** June 3–18, 2026
- **Meaning:** the idea gained its first complete shape

Aide started as a chat app, then grew into Alles: a launcher, shared search, files, mail, photos,
calendar, tasks, documents, and other personal tools. The formal plans in this stage audited that first
body app by app and ended with the first broad regression pass.

## Outcome

The stage established the first usable multi-app form. Its regression record says the planned tasks
were delivered, but later stages replaced several designs and changed many data paths.

Use these files as history and implementation evidence, not as the current product direction.
