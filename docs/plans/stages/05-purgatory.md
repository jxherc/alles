# Stage 5 — Purgatory

- **Status:** historical transition; not a clean release
- **Period:** June 28–July 10, 2026
- **Meaning:** Alles was useful, but its branches and product directions no longer formed one trusted whole

This stage contained heavy Photos work, the Obsidian/Markdown vault shift, QA, deployment thinking, and
branch consolidation attempts. Some work existed only locally or on separate branches. Older plans also
disagreed about Docs, Photos, navigation, and the frontend platform.

Purgatory is why Afterlife starts with recovery, backups, compatibility, and a clean product map instead
of another feature wave.

## Main records

- `alles-road-report.md` — large local audit snapshot.
- `docs/roadmap/` — smaller local roadmap wiki.
- `qa-status.md` — local QA and handoff record.
- [Current shipped specifications](../../../specifications.md) — still the authority for behavior that
  exists now.

The first three records are local working documents. Some are ignored or untracked and may not exist in
every clone.

## Major threads

- consolidate `dev`, `notes-vault`, and Photos work;
- make Markdown files the Docs/Notes source of truth;
- protect locked Journal content from search leakage;
- turn Gallery into a real local Photos experience;
- prove performance before home-server deployment;
- finish browser QA instead of trusting unit tests alone;
- decide which earlier architecture experiments to keep.

## Exit

Purgatory ends when the project stops treating its pile of old roadmaps as one active plan. Afterlife is
the new active direction. Purgatory records remain useful evidence, but they do not control future work.
