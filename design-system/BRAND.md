# brand

## visual idea

**one quiet workbench**

Alles should feel calm, precise, connected, private, and capable. It is a daily tool, not a marketing page or a collection of unrelated dashboards.

## character

- calm, not lifeless;
- compact, not cramped;
- direct, not cold;
- capable, not showy;
- personal, not cute by default;
- technical only where the information is technical.

## signature

The signature is structural: every finished app owns its identity and Home path, aligns local context
with its work surface, and uses the same terse status language and precise control geometry. The shared
grammar must not become a repeated `<app> / alles` breadcrumb above otherwise app-owned interfaces.
Specialist data remains the visual focus.
The app name is never repeated as a giant landing title beneath that shell.

## palette

Use near-black or warm off-white surfaces, warm neutral text, quiet grays, and restrained Alles purple for focus and meaningful active state. Orange is permission or high-authority state, red is failure or destructive action, and green is confirmed success. Do not spray the accent across headings, icons, and buttons.

## type and icons

Use the local/system interface stack. Do not make a downloaded trend font the product voice. Use tabular figures for data and monospace only for real code, paths, terminal output, or identifiers. Icons are bare, consistent, and useful; labels are preferred when clearer.

## avoid

- gradients, glows, glass, floating cards, hover lift, and decorative motion;
- giant rounded cards, excessive pills, icon tiles, and card-everything layouts;
- cool blue-charcoal as a default dark surface;
- marketing headlines inside specialist tools;
- oversized app-name headings repeated below the app shell;
- fake app windows, invented logos, or decorative data;
- many unrelated border, radius, sidebar, toolbar, or empty-state systems;
- content hidden behind entrance animation;
- theme controls outside Settings.

## references

Use the approved KOKUEN app starters and the real product as behavior references. Other products may inform placement or flow, but never copy their content, palette, or complete layout.
