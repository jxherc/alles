# principles

1. Solve the user task before styling the surface.
2. Keep one quiet workbench across the product.
3. Preserve specialist behavior; unify the interaction grammar.
4. Reuse and compose before inventing a component.
5. Use semantic tokens instead of isolated values.
6. Use hierarchy and grouping before boxes, borders, or decoration.
7. Give every visual difference a reason.
8. Keep real content readable; do not design around placeholder rectangles.
9. Show system status and the consequence of every action.
10. Make errors short, local, and recoverable.
11. Design loading, empty, partial, offline, permission, long-content, and interrupted states.
12. Let content and task priority determine layout.
13. Use whitespace to group, not to create empty dead zones.
14. Keep familiar behavior unless evidence supports changing it.
15. Reflow for smaller space instead of shrinking everything.
16. Keep content visible even when motion or JavaScript fails.
17. Use motion for feedback or continuity, never decoration alone.
18. Keep AI state honest: provider, model, permission, progress, and evidence stay understandable.
19. Match KOKUEN and the product, not a current UI trend.
20. A clean screen is the floor. It still needs one clear idea tied to the task.
